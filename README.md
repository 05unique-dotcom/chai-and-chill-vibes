## 🍵 About

**Chai & Chill** is a warm, appetizing Indian street food landing page inspired by the iconic street food culture of Mumbai. From cutting chai to vada pav — every desi comfort food you love, in one place.

> *"Your favorite street food, one sip away"*
> — Est. 1985 · Mumbai Style 🇮🇳

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Hero Section** — Real chai background with floating cup animations
- 🛒 **Working Cart** — Add to cart with live counter & toast notifications  
- 🍽️ **Menu Cards** — 6 items with real food images & prices in ₹
- 🔥 **Special Offer Banner** — Animated limited time deals
- 📖 **About Section** — "From Mumbai Streets to Your Heart"
- 📞 **Contact Section** — Address, phone, location
- 📱 **Mobile Responsive** — Works perfectly on all screen sizes
- 🎨 **CSS Animations** — Hover effects, fade-ins, bounce animations
